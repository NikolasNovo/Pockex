Delete Account & Data

App: Pockex
Support: info.pockex@gmail.com
Last updated: February 21, 2026

Summary: Most of your content in Pockex is stored locally on your device. You can delete local app data instantly from inside the app (recommended). If you signed in with Apple/Google for Cloud Backup and want any associated identifiers removed, you can also request deletion by email.

1) What “account” means in Pockex

Pockex does not require a separate username/password account. If you choose to sign in, it is done via Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In and is used only for:

  • Cloud Backup / restore (if enabled in the app)
  • Helping validate subscription entitlements (via the app stores and RevenueCat)

Payments and subscription billing are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. Pockex does not receive your full payment card details.

2) Delete data inside the app (fastest)

In the Pockex app, go to:

  • Profile -> Preferences -> Delete Account

This action deletes your local Pockex content stored on the device (notes, lists, settings, caches) and signs you out. This is irreversible.

3) Delete account/data without the app (web option)

If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion by emailing: info.pockex@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Subject: Pockex Deletion Request
  • The sign-in method you used: Apple or Google
  • The email address used for sign-in (Apple private relay email is OK)
  • (Optional) A screenshot of the app’s “Account” screen showing your sign-in status, if available

4) What will be deleted

  • Local device data: your app content stored on the device (deleted via in-app deletion or by uninstalling).
  • Sign-in association (if applicable): our records linking your Apple/Google sign-in identifier to Pockex usage (if we have any).
  • Subscription identifiers (if applicable): we will remove or anonymize identifiers we control that are used to check entitlement status, where feasible.

5) What cannot be deleted by us

Some information is controlled by the platform stores:

  • App Store / Google Play purchase history and billing records are managed by Apple/Google and may be retained per their policies and legal obligations.

6) Important: canceling a subscription

Deleting app data does not automatically cancel your store subscription. To stop future billing, cancel through your store account:

  • Google Play: open Google Play -> Payments & subscriptions -> Subscriptions -> Pockex -> Cancel
  • Apple App Store: Settings (iOS) -> Apple ID -> Subscriptions -> Pockex -> Cancel

7) Processing time

We aim to process deletion requests within a reasonable time. If we need additional details to verify your request, we will reply from info.pockex@gmail.com.

8) Contact

If you have questions about deletion or privacy, contact: info.pockex@gmail.com
